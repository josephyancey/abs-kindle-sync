# Audiobookshelf Sync

Syncs books and reading position between [Audiobookshelf](https://www.audiobookshelf.org/) and Amazon's own Kindle reader (not KOReader).

- Copies every Audiobookshelf book that has an AZW3 ebook into your Kindle library.
- Saves your reading position to Audiobookshelf as you read.
- When you open a book or wake the Kindle, offers to move to your latest Audiobookshelf position.

After installing, open **Audiobookshelf Sync** from your library to sign in and pick libraries.

**Alpha:** tested only on a Paperwhite 5 with firmware 5.18.5. Needs firmware 5.16.3 or newer, an Audiobookshelf server the Kindle can reach, and books with an AZW3 (plus the EPUB, for position sync) in Audiobookshelf.

Uninstalling leaves the synced books in documents/Audiobookshelf.
